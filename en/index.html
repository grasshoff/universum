Famulus in brief

The same three times, at growing length. The wording is that of the canonical account.

In one sentence

Famulus is AI that shows its grounds: every answer is the conclusion of an argument from addressed evidence, and what the evidence does not carry is marked as not validated.

In one paragraph

It is a new kind of scholarly assistance: not an answer generator but a knowledge ground. Every answer is the conclusion of an argument from addressed evidence; what the evidence does not carry is marked as not validated. Famulus serves science, cultural heritage and a society's understanding of itself. The name comes from the history of scholarship: the famulus prepares material, counts, orders and checks – and does not judge. Judgement stays with the scholar.

The core

Validation follows the standards of best explanation and justification of the discipline concerned. This is the necessary core of everything in Famulus – agents, rule sets, reference collections and every expert. Famulus sets no truth standard of its own; it makes a field's standards explicit, checkable and revisable, and applies them proposition by proposition.

Chapter II

Validation

Justified · Validated sentence by sentence

An answer is the conclusion of an argument: addressed evidence, a declared chain of reasoning and its validation. A sentence is factually validated when the propositions it expresses follow from registered grounds, none is refuted and none contradicts an earlier sentence or a register. Every other sentence is not validated – no verdict of falsity.

Chapter V

Publication

Publication with a quality seal

A notebook becomes an issue of the series Famulus Expertise (published by OpenScienceTechnology, open access). An issue carries the quality seal when three necessary conditions hold: it has completed the pass through the Famulus Lektor, in which the author agrees explicitly to every thesis, every premise and every step of the proof; every statement about a source rests on a sharp Famulus reference as grounding evidence; and the methodological criteria of argumentative correctness are fulfilled, measured by quality gates.

State and limits

The delimitations of the canonical account. “In use” means: in use in the project's own network. “Future work” means: described and intended, not built.

  • in use
  • built
  • draft
  • future work
  • perspective
  • public
  • in the own network
  • not published
  • No claim to validity from retrieval. A sentence that was found shows that it stands in the collection, not that what it asserts is true.
  • “Not validated” does not mean “false”. Missing evidence justifies nothing and refutes nothing.
  • No truth standard of its own. Famulus makes a field's standards of best explanation and justification explicit, checkable and revisable; it does not replace them.
  • Only propositions are true or false. Entailment and contradiction hold between propositions, not between sentences.
  • No assurance without a basis. Data protection and legal conformity are properties of the design, not a certification; federation, external experts, literature watch and further method modules are future work, not holdings.
  • The quality seal is no guarantee of truth. It says that an issue has passed a procedure, that its statements about sources can be checked against sharp references and that the gates of the series are fulfilled. The author alone is responsible for the statements; an objection that convinces is annotated in a new version.
  • WWK is a perspective, not a holding. The address order, the register without a centre and the addresses of notebooks are built and in use in the project's own network. A worldwide network of other reference points does not exist; how fast it grows is an expectation, not a measurement. “Examined by the community” means: open to criticism and justified by the standards of the field, not: infallible.
  • Judgement stays with the expert. Famulus prepares, checks and names limits; it does not decide.
