Famulus in brief

Famulus in three versions: one sentence, one paragraph, the core. The wording is that of the canonical account.

In one sentence

Famulus is AI that shows its grounds: every answer is the conclusion of an argument from addressed evidence, and what the evidence does not carry is marked as not validated.

In one paragraph

Famulus is a new kind of scholarly assistance. Specialised agents carry out proven methods of research – finding, reading, dating, counting, comparing, reconstructing, checking – on holdings made accessible in which every document, every page and every passage has a permanent address. An answer is the conclusion of an argument from this evidence; its grounds are open to view, and every sentence of a text is checked for whether it is factually validated. The rules of inference and of use are registered and revisable, not hidden in the weights of a model. The data stay at the level of protection their owners choose. Famulus organises research openly and critically, as science does, and draws on an open network of experts with checked specialist knowledge – judgement stays with the scholar.

The core

Validation follows the standards of best explanation and justification of the discipline concerned. This is the necessary core of everything in Famulus – agents, rule sets, reference collections and every expert. Famulus sets no truth standard of its own; it makes a field's standards explicit, checkable and revisable, and applies them proposition by proposition. What counts as evidence in philology, as a measurement with an error model in astronomy and as a finding on the object in archaeology counts in Famulus in the same way.

Eight functions: what Famulus does for knowledge

Each function answers one question and is determined by a definition, a criterion and a limit (draft of 20 Nov 2026, to be confirmed by the author). It is divided into stages; each stage states its result and its state. The order is the order of processing: a source is grounded before it is understood; a result is justified before it is described, and described before it is published.

Function 1

Grounding

Famulus Reference: the primary evidence of the documents

Which evidence justifies a proposition?

Definition
Grounding makes a source citable: each of its objects – document, page, sentence, figure, table cell, person – receives a permanent address and a record of its provenance, and an object with a wording also a checked reading of that wording.
Criterion
An object is grounded if and only if its address resolves to exactly this object and, where it has a wording, the reading of that wording has passed the gate of its stage. A citation is composed only from recorded bibliographic data; where a field is missing, the citation states the gap.
Limit
A sentence that was found shows that it stands in the collection, not that what it asserts is true.

Function 2

Understanding

LWgrammar: language competence relative to author, work and time

What does the wording mean in its context?

Definition
Understanding determines which proposition the wording of a passage expresses. It assigns to every word its base form and its place in the sentence, and to every expression the sense it has in the usage of its author, its work and its time.
Criterion
The rules of use are registered, citable and revisable. Every reading names the passage, the rule, the degree of evidence and the grounds.
Limit
A reading determines which proposition a sentence expresses; it does not determine whether that proposition is true.

Function 3

Calculating and modelling

Famulus experts and instruments of reasoning: each operates an instrument by its rules

Which values and propositions can be derived from the tables, models and measurements of a source?

Definition
Calculating and modelling derives values and propositions from numbers, tables and models that a source attests. Calculations use the table, the number system and the rounding of the source. A model is a complex proposition about an abstract object, such as a construction or a planetary model whose instances are the motions of the planets.
Criterion
Every step of a calculation receives a verdict against the printed value: it agrees, it differs by a stated amount, or it cannot be recomputed. An expert counts as checked when its computations are measured against documented comparisons.
Limit
A computed table never takes the place of an attested one; a modern computation stands beside it only as a parallel. A model without attested parameters yields no value.

Function 4

Justifying and checking

Famulus Proof, Famulus Lektor and the method institution

Does the proposition follow, and does it stand up to examination?

Definition
Justifying presents an answer as the conclusion of an argument whose premises are grounded propositions and registered rules. Checking establishes whether the conclusion follows from the premises and whether the answer to a critical question put to the same data refutes it. Entailment holds between propositions, not between sentences.
Criterion
A sentence is factually validated if and only if the propositions it expresses follow from the propositions of registered evidence, none of them is refuted and none contradicts a proposition expressed by an earlier sentence or a register.
Limit
Every other sentence with factual content is not validated; this is the contradictory negation, not a verdict of falsity. A false premise makes an argument unsound; its conclusion loses its justification, but it does not thereby become false.

Function 5

Working

Notebook and Harness: question, score, answer, check and provenance in one block

Where are question, procedure and result recorded together?

Definition
Working keeps a question, the procedure that answers it and the result together in one document, the Famulus Notebook. A block states the question, calls the score (Partitur) that describes the procedure, and records answer, check and provenance.
Criterion
Every result has an address and a trace: which tool, which model and which instruction performed which step. A block contains a verdict against an independent expectation.
Limit
A specialised agent (Fachkraft) carries out a procedure; it does not judge. Judgement stays with the scholar.

Function 6

Describing

Rules of composition, form of presentation and presentation elements

How is a result put into words and forms?

Definition
Describing puts a justified result into wording and form: the outline of the argument, the sentences, the terms and the presentation elements – table, figure, diagram, animation, 3D scene. Describing is the reverse of understanding: understanding goes by rules of use from wording to proposition, describing by rules of composition from proposition to wording.
Criterion
A description names the grounds that justify it. Every presentation element reads its numbers from addressed data, its caption says what is to be seen, and it counts only once it has been rendered and inspected.
Limit
Describing adds no proposition that is not justified.

Function 7

Publishing

Famulus Expertise: publication with a quality seal

How does a result become citable and open to critique?

Definition
Publishing makes a text a publication: it has a permanent address and a version, states the conditions under which its author answers for it, and admits objections on record. In Famulus this is the series Famulus Expertise.
Criterion
An issue carries the quality seal if and only if it has completed the pass through the Famulus Lektor with the author's assent to every thesis, every assumption and every step, every proposition about a source rests on a sharp Famulus reference as primary evidence, the quality gates are fulfilled, and the author has released this version.
Limit
The quality seal is no guarantee of truth; the author alone is responsible for the propositions. An objection that stands up to examination is annotated at the point concerned in a new version.

Function 8

Connecting

WWK – World-Wide Knowledge: registers without a central authority

How do holdings connect?

Definition
Connecting makes the addresses of one institution resolvable for any other, without a central authority: every reference point publishes a signed register of the namespaces it holds, and a resolver answers an address at the reference point that holds it. External agents and library catalogues reach the same objects through fixed interfaces.
Criterion
An address resolves, no matter which reference point holds it. A proposition holds not because an authority declares it true, but as far as its grounds lie open and stand up to examination by the scientific community.
Limit
Reference points of other institutions do not exist yet; the WWK is a perspective, not a state already reached.

Famulus Science: modules for research

Famulus Science is a group of modules that are to support the working process of research. The cards reproduce the description of the page Famulus Science; that page records no state for any module. Every card names the functions the module uses.

  • Research agenda and project management

    From the research goal to stages and milestones, every step logged. You always see where your project stands and are told early about additional work that is needed.

    uses 5 Working4 Justifying and checking

  • Hypothesis and agenda monitor

    Your own monitor of the research process: hypotheses with their state of justification, agenda and milestones, and every morning what changed overnight.

    uses 4 Justifying and checking5 Working

  • not yet built

    Nightly research screening

    Every night, starting from your newly obtained results, Famulus checks world research for relevant connections: confirmation, supporting evidence or contradiction of your own findings.

    uses 1 Grounding4 Justifying and checking

  • Literature

    Your library as a searchable, citable collection. Missing titles are ordered, their arrival tracked, and the work slotted into your research.

    uses 1 Grounding

  • Structural analysis of publications

    A publication taken apart into its claims, its argument, its causal inferences, its empirical findings and its method, with a justified assessment of every step.

    uses 2 Understanding4 Justifying and checking

  • Hypothesis management

    All competing hypotheses with their state of justification. Famulus proposes the experiment that best separates them.

    uses 4 Justifying and checking

  • Lab notebook

    Dictate, photograph, enter: experiments, findings and thoughts become a structured lab notebook that keeps the hypotheses up to date.

    uses 5 Working1 Grounding

  • Lab and instrument management

    Instruments, measuring time, materials and maintenance in view. If an instrument is booked or material runs short, Famulus plans the schedule around it.

    uses 5 Working

  • Analytical bench

    Data become minimal theories: which factors are jointly causally relevant, which alternative routes exist, and where the data support no verdict.

    uses 3 Calculating and modelling4 Justifying and checking

  • Publication management and reports

    From the manuscript draft through submission, reviews and revision to the project and funder report, grounded in your own entries and sources.

    uses 6 Describing7 Publishing

  • Research group

    The results of the whole group are analysed and built into one overall work plan. Tasks go to the people involved automatically; feedback comes back to your workspace, prepared.

    uses 5 Working8 Connecting

  • Custom training

    You bring in your own research documents – publications, lab notebooks, data, proposals – and Famulus adapts to the requirements of your research field.

    uses 3 Calculating and modelling2 Understanding

Fields of application

A field of application uses a selection of the eight functions. It contributes what belongs to it alone: its holdings (function 1), its rules of use (2) and its experts (3). Justifying and checking, working, describing, publishing and connecting are the same for all fields. The state of a field is the lowest state of the components it contributes.

State and limits

The delimitations of the canonical account. State of a component: “runs” means built and in regular use within the project's own computer network; “built” means built, not yet in regular use; “partly built” means that part of the stage is built; “draft” means begun, not completed; “not yet built” means described and intended, not built; “perspective” means an expectation, not a project with a state. Access: “public page” means that a page about it can be reached under famulus.lettre.ai; “accessible only within the project's computer network” means that there is no public page.

  • No claim to validity from retrieval. A sentence that was found shows that it stands in the collection, not that what it asserts is true.
  • “Not validated” does not mean “false”. Missing evidence justifies nothing and refutes nothing.
  • No truth standard of its own. Famulus makes the standards of best explanation and justification of a field explicit, checkable and revisable; it does not replace them.
  • Only propositions are true or false. Entailment and contradiction hold between propositions, not between sentences.
  • No assurance without a basis. Data protection and legal conformity are properties of the design, not a certification; federation, external experts, literature watch and further method modules are not yet built; they are not in place.
  • The quality seal is no guarantee of truth. It says that an issue has passed a procedure, that its propositions about sources can be checked against sharp references and that the gates of the series are fulfilled. The author alone is responsible for the propositions; an objection that convinces is annotated in a new version.
  • WWK is a perspective, not a state reached. The address scheme, the register without a centre and the addresses of notebooks are built and in use within the project's computer network. A worldwide network of other reference points does not exist; how fast it grows is an expectation, not a measurement. “Examined by the community” means: open to criticism and justified by the standards of the field, not: infallible.
  • Judgement stays with the specialist. Famulus prepares, checks and names limits; it does not decide.
